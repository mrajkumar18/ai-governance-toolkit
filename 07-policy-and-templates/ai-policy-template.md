## 1. Purpose
Enable the safe, lawful and beneficial use of AI, including generative and agentic AI, while managing risks to people, the organisation and society.

## 2. Scope
All AI systems that are built, bought, embedded in vendor products, or used by staff and contractors, including pilots, proofs of concept, and free or personal tools used for work.

## 3. Principles
1. **Human accountability:** a named person is accountable for every AI system and its outcomes.
2. **Fairness and non-discrimination:** AI must not unfairly disadvantage individuals or groups.
3. **Transparency:** people are told when AI is used and can obtain meaningful information.
4. **Privacy and data protection:** personal data is minimised, lawfully used and protected.
5. **Safety, security and resilience:** systems are tested, secured and monitored; humans can intervene.
6. **Proportionality:** controls scale with risk (see risk tiers).
7. **Contestability:** affected people can question outcomes and obtain human review.

## 4. Requirements
1. Every AI use case **must be registered** and risk-tiered before build or procurement (Gate 0). Pilots are in scope.
2. Tier 0 uses are **prohibited**.
3. Tier 1-2 uses require design approval, impact assessment, pre-deployment testing and ongoing monitoring per the lifecycle gates.
4. **Human oversight** is required for decisions with significant effect on individuals. Fully automated significant decisions require Committee approval.
5. **Agents** must operate within an approved autonomy level, tool allow-list and least-privilege permissions, with a tested kill switch (L2 and above).
6. **Third-party AI** must pass vendor assessment; contracts must cover data use, retention, audit rights, incident notification and IP.
7. **Personal data** must not be sent to external AI services without an approved lawful basis, contract and transfer assessment.
8. **Incidents and near misses** must be reported and handled per the AI incident playbook.
9. **Staff** who build, approve or use AI must complete role-appropriate training.
10. **Exceptions** require documented risk acceptance (see exceptions process).

## 5. Roles
See the RACI in `docs/roles-and-raci.md`.

## 6. Compliance and consequences
Monitoring is by the AI Governance Lead and Internal Audit. Breaches may lead to suspension of the system and disciplinary action.
