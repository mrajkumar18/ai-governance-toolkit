# Generative AI Acceptable-Use Standard (Template)

A short standard to control **shadow AI** while the full program is built.

## Approved tools
Only tools on the **approved list** may be used for work. Maintain the list in the [AI register](../02-inventory/ai-system-register.csv). Personal accounts on public AI tools are **not** approved for work data.

## Never enter into non-approved tools
- Personal data of customers, employees or partners
- Confidential, commercial, legal, financial or strategic information
- Source code, credentials, keys, internal system details
- Information covered by NDA or regulatory secrecy

## You may (with approved tools)
- Draft, summarise, translate and brainstorm using **non-confidential** content
- Use approved enterprise tools according to their data-classification limits

## You must
1. **Verify outputs** before relying on them; you remain accountable.
2. **Disclose** AI-generated content where required by policy, law or client terms.
3. **Not** use AI for decisions about individuals (hiring, credit, discipline, performance) unless approved through the AI gates.
4. **Check IP and licensing** before publishing AI-generated content externally.
5. **Report** suspected leaks, harmful outputs or misuse immediately (see the incident playbook).
6. **Not** build or connect agents, plugins or automations to company systems without registration and approval.

## Registration and discovery
- Register any AI tool, feature, plugin or agent you use or enable for work, including free tools, pilots and AI features inside software we already own.
- The organisation may use network, identity, endpoint, procurement and code-scanning data to identify AI use (see the [shadow-AI discovery guide](../docs/shadow-ai-discovery.md)).
- Use declared during the amnesty window is assessed through the gates without disciplinary action, unless it involved deliberate disregard of data-protection or confidentiality rules.

## Consequences
Violations may be treated as policy or data-protection breaches.
