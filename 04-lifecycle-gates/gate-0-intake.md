# Gate 0: Intake

**Purpose:** Decide whether the use case may proceed, and how.
**Applies to:** All tiers. **Owner:** Business owner. **Approver:** see [approver matrix](README.md).

## Entry criteria
- [ ] [Intake form](../01-intake-and-risk-tiering/ai-use-case-intake-form.md) completed
- [ ] Business and technical owners named

## Checklist
- [ ] Stop checks (Section A) answered; no prohibited practice (TK-03)
- [ ] High-risk domains (Section B) answered; Legal consulted if any "Yes" or if unsure (TK-03)
- [ ] Risk tier assigned and recorded with rationale (TK-03)
- [ ] Autonomy level declared; L4 flagged for Committee (TK-21)
- [ ] Jurisdictions, EU nexus and legal role (provider / deployer) recorded; sector regulators identified (see [applicability guide](../docs/applicability-guide.md))
- [ ] Pilots and experiments confirmed in scope: **no pilot is exempt** from intake
- [ ] Entry created in the [register](../02-inventory/ai-system-register.csv) (TK-02)
- [ ] Preliminary data and vendor concerns noted (TK-06, TK-15)
- [ ] Owners have completed required AI literacy training (TK-20)

## Required evidence
Completed intake form · Questionnaire with tier · Register entry · Legal note (if applicable)

## Outcomes
| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Approve | Proceed to G1 (Tier 1-2) or G2 (Tier 3) |
| Approve with conditions | Proceed; conditions tracked in the register |
| Reject | Tier 0, or business case / risk unacceptable |
